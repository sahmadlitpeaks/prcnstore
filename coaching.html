FAQ

Common questions.

Don't see yours? Talk to us.

Is this medical care or coaching?

Our coaches are board-certified clinicians, but coaching is non-diagnostic and complements (rather than replaces) your primary doctor. We'll always refer you on if you need acute care.

Can I do everything online?

Yes. All sessions are available via secure video. In-person is optional and based in our Dubai Marina clinic.

Do you support insurance?

We can issue a clinic invoice for out-of-network reimbursement. We don't direct-bill insurers at this time.

Do I need a Precision Chex™ test first?

No — you can start with just a session. Adding a test makes the first plan more precise.

Languages?

English and Arabic.

Refunds?

Full refund if cancelled 24+ hours before your first session. After that, plans are non-refundable but transferable to a partner.
