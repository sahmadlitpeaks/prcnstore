Pain Ease™ is a multi-pathway formula designed by our medical board for sustained, drug-free relief from chronic and inflammatory pain. It pairs PEA (palmitoylethanolamide) — a naturally occurring fatty-acid amide that calms overactive immune cells in the nervous system — with three of the most-studied botanicals in the inflammation literature: high-bioavailability curcumin, ginger root extract and boswellia serrata.

Unlike conventional analgesics, Pain Ease™ doesn't blunt symptoms in isolation; it dampens the underlying signalling pathways so pain backs off and stays off.
